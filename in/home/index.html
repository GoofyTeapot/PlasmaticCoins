Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start collecting?

Begin with circulated coins, learn grading basics, and explore affordable sets.

Do you buy coins?

Yes — visit our Contact page to submit photos and details.

How do I store coins safely?

Use flips, tubes, or slabs and avoid touching coins with bare hands.

What are key dates?

Coins with low mintages or rare varieties that collectors seek out.

Rare Penny Checklist

Key dates and varieties every collector should know — from 1909-S VDB to 1955 DDO.

Key Date Pennies

Some pennies were minted in extremely low quantities, making them highly valuable. Must‑know dates include the 1909‑S VDB, 1914‑D, and 1931‑S.

Famous Doubled Dies

Dramatic doubled dies like the 1955 DDO, 1969‑S DDO, and 1972 DDO are among the most sought‑after varieties in the hobby.

Other Valuable Varieties

Look for repunched mintmarks, missing initials, and transitional errors. Popular finds include the 1922 No D and the 1983 DDR.

Coin Roll Hunting

Learn strategies for finding silver, wheat cents, and rare varieties in bank rolls.

Finding Silver

Search half dollar rolls for 90% silver (1964) and 40% silver (1965–1970). Customer-wrapped rolls offer the best chance of silver finds.

Wheat Cents

Penny rolls often contain wheat cents, especially older customer-wrapped rolls. Look for pre-1959 designs and copper pennies from 1959–1982.

Rare Varieties

Keep an eye out for doubled dies, repunched mint marks, and missing initials. Popular finds include the 1972 DDO, 1982-P No FG, and 1995 DDO.

How to Store Coins

Protect your collection using flips, tubes, albums, and proper handling techniques.

Use Proper Holders

Store coins in 2×2 flips, capsules, or tubes to prevent scratches and exposure to air. Avoid PVC holders, which can damage coins over time.

Handle Coins Correctly

Always hold coins by the edges to avoid fingerprints. Wearing cotton or nitrile gloves helps prevent accidental damage.

Organize & Label

Keep your collection organized in boxes or albums. Label each coin with its date, mintmark, grade, and any special notes.

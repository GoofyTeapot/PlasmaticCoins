Understanding Mintmarks

A quick guide to identifying mintmarks and what they mean for coin rarity.

What Are Mintmarks?

Mintmarks are small letters on coins that show where they were made. Common U.S. mintmarks include P (Philadelphia), D (Denver), S (San Francisco), and W (West Point).

Why Mintmarks Matter

Some mints produced fewer coins in certain years, making those mintmarked coins more valuable. For example, low‑mintage San Francisco coins can be highly collectible.

Where to Find Them

Mintmarks are usually found on the obverse (front) near the date, but older coins may place them on the reverse. Learning each coin type’s mintmark location helps you spot rare varieties quickly.
